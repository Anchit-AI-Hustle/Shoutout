# 01 — Product

## What ShoutOut is

An all-in-one platform for the independent music industry in India.

**ShoutOut is the brand name.** It is not a data model or an object type. The product is eight modules sharing one account, one payment rail and one set of services — the way Shopify is Products, Orders, Customers, Payments and Marketing.

## The problem

Indian live music is growing fast, and none of the infrastructure exists.

- Artists wait 30–90 days for money. Sometimes it never comes.
- Session players get paid last, least, or not at all.
- Managers run entire careers on WhatsApp and Google Sheets — no pipeline, no calendar, no settlement tracking.
- Producers find vocalists through Instagram DMs, or not at all.
- Splits are agreed verbally and discovered years later, when there is money and a fight.
- Nobody knows what to charge, because no price reference exists.

Every one of these is a module below.

## The eight modules

### 1. Profiles

Be findable and bookable.

One account holds many roles at once — a person is an artist, a producer and a manager simultaneously, not one of them chosen at signup. Structured attributes per role (vocal range, BPM comfort, languages, instruments, genres). Auto-generated EPK, public and indexable.

### 2. Gigs

Get booked, and book acts.

All circuits: weddings, corporate, college fests, clubs, festivals, folk and devotional, classical, government and tourism. Brief intake → shortlist → quote priced off real data → hold that actually binds → contract → rider → settled.

### 3. Collabs

Find people to make music with.

A producer needs a vocalist. Or a songwriter _and_ a vocalist, independently, on different terms. A guitarist needs a drummer to form a band. An artist needs a tour manager and a sound engineer for a run. A rapper needs a mix engineer. Work rooms with versioned stems, and split sheets signed before any file moves.

### 4. Studio

Make music, and describe the sound you want.

Generation, reference tracks to attach to a collab request, stem separation, BPM/key/genre detection on every upload, sync-length variants. Assembled from two existing assets — the MusicGenAI semantic engine and the Third Eye nine-stage pipeline.

### 5. Stage

Compete and get discovered.

Season shows and city leagues across all genres — hip-hop, electronic, indie, Punjabi, folk, classical crossover, devotional. Modelled on MTV Hustle and Wild 'N Out. The audition funnel is the artist-acquisition engine: every entrant who does not make it still gets a scorecard, a free EPK, and open gigs in their city.

### 6. Payments

Get paid, and pay everyone.

Contracts with eSign, splits, settlements, payouts with UTR tracking, TDS and GST computed and shown before signature. **Session players are paid on the same rail at the same time as everyone else.**

### 7. Promotion

Grow an audience and sell to it.

Website acquisition funnels, programmatic city and genre pages, release campaigns, fan lists the artist owns, WhatsApp lifecycle sequences, sponsorship matching.

### 8. Credits

Own your work.

Split sheets from Collabs write here automatically. A verified record of who made what, attached to a person, exportable for PRO registration. No separate data entry, ever.

## Personas

| Persona                      | Primary job                                          | Pays?                         |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Artist manager**           | Keep 3–15 artists booked, priced right, paid on time | **Yes — the paying customer** |
| Independent artist           | Get found, get booked, own the audience              | No — monetised via the rail   |
| Producer / writer / engineer | Find collaborators, agree splits, get paid           | No — monetised via the rail   |
| Organiser / promoter         | Find the right act, be certain they turn up          | Take rate                     |
| Wedding / corporate planner  | Vetted talent, contract, recourse                    | Take rate                     |
| Venue                        | Fill the calendar with acts that draw                | Take rate                     |
| Brand / sponsor              | Reach a music audience with provable numbers         | 15% of sponsorship            |
| Label A&R                    | Signal, not vibes                                    | Data seat                     |

A self-managed artist is an org with a roster of one and commission zero. **Serve `n=1` as first-class, never as a degraded case.** The path from one artist to five must need no migration.

## Why one platform and not eight products

Because the modules feed each other, and separately none of them wins.

```
Stage auditions  →  Profiles fill
Profiles         →  Collabs and Gigs have supply
Collabs          →  Credits populate automatically
Gigs settled     →  price index sharpens  →  better quotes
Studio           →  better collab briefs, and sync-ready catalogue
Payments         →  the data that underwrites financing later
Promotion        →  fan lists that price sponsorship
```

A competitor can copy one module. Copying the platform means building all eight and wiring them together, and the wiring is the part that compounds.

## Success metrics

North star: **settled money on the rail**.

| Branch  | Metric                                    | Target   |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Supply  | Audition rejection → profile complete     | **>60%** |
| Demand  | Brief → shortlist opened                  | >75%     |
| Demand  | Shortlist → hold placed                   | >35%     |
| Money   | Payout SLA met (T+2)                      | >97%     |
| Money   | Reconciliation errors                     | **0**    |
| Money   | Off-platform leakage                      | <20%     |
| Credits | Collabs closing with a signed split sheet | 100%     |

## Out of scope

Streaming distribution · ticketing · label services · publishing administration · a social feed. Each is a different company.
